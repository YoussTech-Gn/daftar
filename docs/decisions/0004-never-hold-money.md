# Decision 0004 — Record money, never move it

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-21

**Context:** Holding or transferring funds requires a payment licence (BCEAO, BCRG, Bank Al-Maghrib…).

**Decision:** Daftar only records credit and payments. Payment methods (Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo)
are labels, not integrations. Reminders open WhatsApp with a pre-filled text; the shopkeeper sends it.

**Consequences:** No licensing risk for the MVP. Still to verify: whether record-only apps fall under
any central-bank rule (open question in the research report).
